_pick through the refractions to find your way ..._<br><br>

> **[[NEVERMORE OVERVIEW.|NEVERMORES]]** : based on hit webcomic nevermore. what if a bunch of people from a few different time periods, mostly in the 1800s, died and had to compete to earn the right to be revived. collaborative with cas.<br>
> **MODERN MISC.** : what it says on the tin. modern with \[mostly] no complications to that fact. most of my ocs have a tangible modern au, if they're not based there already.<br>
> **[[MMVERSE/index|MMVERSE.]]** : a joint ocverse with moon, featuring a fictionalised pantheon and afterlives. <br>
> **[[TO SCORCH THE EARTH/index|TO SCORCH THE EARTH.]]** : medieval-esque high -> low fantasy with a small bunch of countries, politics, and religions. in-part collaborative with moon and cas but most of the worldbuilding is my creation. <br>
> **[[PRISM CORPS/index|PRISM CORPS.]]** : the worst best employer in all of the multiverse! eldritch corporate horror under that shiny coat of paint, prism corps. and all the distant divisions work in service of the relentless pursuit of information.<br>
> **[[RIGS OVERVIEW.|TO THE WORLD'S EDGE.]]** : _inspired by still wakes the deep._ world covered in water with above-water 'rigs' and below-water creatures that evolve erratically in the span of months or weeks. accompanied by the tower!<br>
> **[[LOVE TIES LOVE BITES/index|LOVE TIES, LOVE BITES !]]** : desire, tangible.<br>
> **[[VOIDTOUCHED/index|VOIDTOUCHED]]** : what remains after the world ends.<br>
> **[[VICES AND VIRTUES/index|VICES & VIRTUES.]]** : a wip death game concept featuring a cast of characters inspired by the cardinal sins and virtues. <br>
> **[[CLUB ZR/index|CLUB ZR.]]** : a club filled with anything the human mind can conjure up that sits in a snug space between reality.  **\[CW: DISCUSSION OF HARD KINK/DARK PARAPHILIA/DD:DNE]**<br>
> **[[EOS]]** : an open, collaborative setting based around self-indulgence and common fantasy/animanga tropes.
