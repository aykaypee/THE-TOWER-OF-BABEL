# OVERVIEW.

- PRISM Corps. \[hereby known as ‘PRISM’] is a multiversal agency specialising in the stabilisation of reality and reality-warping tears, and the identification, categorisation, and containment of anomalous beings within these realities for the benefit of the multiverse as a whole.
- They have locations in 99.998% of local realities — no matter how small their influence may be. PRISM is a household name to some, and a meaningless term to others. As it should be.

PRISM. corps is a multi-universal construct that comprises of 9 departments, with the supposedly unifying goal of wanting to catalogue the entirety of existence and nonexistence alike. to this aim they claim to work similarly to nature researchers, intervening, taking, only when needs must. there are few who know the true scope of prism -- perhaps only the paired executives -- and what it does, with each department being so large, a company within a company itself. the culture reflects this- certain people are simply made for certain jobs, and there's a place for everyone in the journey to soothe reality-warping tears, or categorise, and perhaps contain, unique anomalous entities and objects.

_knowledge is power._

prism is dubiously good-natured, in this way -- the amount of timelines they've saved from destruction and brought to that same end are about even, though they'd like to claim otherwise. 'necessary casulties', one might say, or 'pressure unrelated to prism inspections caused it to buckle under itself'. cureently, they hold some sort of presence in 99.998% of local realities in some shape or form, large or small- with some locations having more than others, being known as a 'core' branch. this typically denotes that something is especially stable abut the current location, or it's closely surrounded by many offshoot timelines or similar realities. the way they \[and i, in the metanarrative i suppose] categorise each different World is by using a hex code model -- each distinct World gets its own hex code \[probably in a way that allows for higher integers than 225], similar ones get similar shades, each timeline variant adds a number code after denominating things like initial stability and amount of deviation from the 'base'. if you're familiar with the triangle agency ttrpg there was a lot of inspo taken from there when i found out what it was.
